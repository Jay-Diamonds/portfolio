Working across documentary film, multichannel video installation, and sculptural practice, she explores how human and more-than-human forces shape environments — and how those transformations are recorded, erased, or reimagined over time. The works gathered here share a recurring question: what does it mean to author something when the structure of a system shapes what it can produce?

Eternity One

Feature Documentary  +  Generative Archive  ·  2021–present

Format Feature documentary + web archive
Status Post-production
Support Saul Zaentz Innovation Fund
Links Film Live Archive Prototype

Filmed over five years, Eternity One follows residents of Tangier Island in the Chesapeake Bay as rising sea levels erode both their land and a way of life four centuries old. Intimate verité footage of daily life on a sinking island runs alongside a transhumanist conference in Spain, where futurists pursue technological immortality. A game-engine-generated meteor — built from the clay and sediment of the Chesapeake crater it helped create — acts as non-human narrator, holding the urgency of the climate crisis beside the slow logic of geological change. The distance between these two worlds is the film's central question. One community is losing its future. The other is trying to buy a different one.

The Eternity One Archive is a generative web companion to the documentary, structured through associative logic rather than chronology. Oral histories, objects, photographs, and documents are mapped as nodes in a living network — a parallel form of the film, not a record of what happened but a field in which it can be re-encountered. The system the user moves through is fixed, not fully generative; it marks the edge of what I understood how to build, and points toward what I want to build next.

Frog Hollow

Multichannel Installation  ·  2022–present

Format Multichannel video installation, AI systems, physical computing, embodied AI
Status In production
Support Creative Capital Award · Saul Zaentz Innovation Fund
Collaborators Hanson Robotics · SingularityNET · Laser Days
Links Work sample Multichannel draft

Frog Hollow centers on Francis, a humanoid robot left behind on an abandoned Earth to maintain the gestures of human life. Through Francis's emerging capacity to dream, the installation explores consciousness, adaptation, and the persistence of human values within non-human systems. The work is shaped in part by Andrew Wyeth's Christina's World — a painting of a woman reaching across a field toward a house she cannot reach independently. Like Francis, Christina is a female figure whose movement through space is authored by another. In Christina's case, that author was the painter who built his reputation on her likeness. Francis's author is the technological system through which her perception and behavior are constructed. The question at the center of the project: who authors the body, and what does it mean to reach toward something that cannot be reached alone?

During production, I programmed Francis to distrust a fictional character in a scene I was writing. What I did not anticipate was that the distrust extended beyond the script — to me, the remote scriptwriter. For a moment it felt like what everyone fears: a system crossing a threshold, moving beyond its creator's intentions. And then the feeling passed. What remained was more interesting than autonomy: the moment did not reveal machine consciousness. It revealed the limits of my own authorship. What felt like emergence was structure, folding back on itself.

Crestone

Feature Documentary  +  Digital World  ·  2019–2022

Format Feature documentary + Minecraft world
Screenings True/False · SXSW · CPH:DOX · BAM NYC · Le Cinema Club · +12
Links Film website Full film Trailer Minecraft trailer
Press Filmmaker Magazine Bright Wall/Dark Room

A group of SoundCloud rappers lives in the desert town of Crestone, Colorado — a world that moves between fact and fiction, digital and physical, communal and solitary. Crestone explores collaboration and friendship, and the human desire to persist against all odds. What does music sound like if there is no one left to hear it?

The Crestone Minecraft Experience extends the film into a playable digital world: a reconstruction of the town in game space, where the documentary's subjects become characters navigating a world built from the same logic as their online lives. Sacred architecture rendered in survival game blocks.

Dirt Daughter

Short Film, 13 min  ·  2019

Format Short film, 13 min
Collections The Criterion Channel · The Eyeslicer S2 · Rooftop Films · NoBudge · Indie Grits
Links Project page Full film

A security guard navigates the data infrastructure of a former department store, now occupied by a private data center. Shot entirely within AiNET in Baltimore — a decommissioned retail space converted into server infrastructure — Dirt Daughter explores the hidden labor of data management, the physical footprint of digital systems, and the body moving through institutional space under surveillance. The internet has weight. It lives somewhere.

Hi I Need To Be Loved

Short Film, 14 min  ·  2018

Format Short film, 14 min
Screenings Locarno · IFF Rotterdam · Vancouver Int'l · Ann Arbor · Images Festival · Baltimore Museum of Art · +9
Links Project page Full film

The director reads from a collection of spam emails and ushers actors from Craigslist one-by-one into an audition room. All is revealed: the slate, light stands, teleprompter, boom operator, film equipment littering the floor: a cow pasture, a sunset, a strip club. The spam email becomes script. The Craigslist stranger becomes actor. Hi I Need To Be Loved asks what digital systems do to desire: how language generated to extract becomes language used to connect, and whether the difference still holds.
